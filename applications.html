Why Join Us?

Professional Training

Receive structured training delivered by experienced instructors covering lifeboat operations, casualty care, communication and command.

Realistic Roleplay

Participate in realistic search and rescue incidents inspired by real RNLI procedures.

Career Progression

Progress through ranks and specialist roles whilst developing your leadership skills.

Available Opportunities

🚤 Lifeboat Crew

Respond to maritime emergencies aboard our rescue vessels.

🩺 Advanced Medic

Deliver emergency medical care and support casualties during incidents.

🏖️ Lifeguard

Patrol beaches, educate the public and respond to emergencies.

⚓ Operations

Coordinate incidents and support operational planning behind the scenes.

🎓 Training Team

Deliver practical and classroom-based training to new recruits.

Minimum Requirements

Requirements

  • Professional attitude.
  • Ability to communicate effectively.
  • Willingness to learn.
  • Follow community rules.
  • Remain active within the organisation.

Recruitment Process

  1. Submit an application.
  2. Application reviewed.
  3. Attend an interview (if required).
  4. Complete initial training.
  5. Begin operational duties.

Ready to Join?
