About Us

The LLP Royal National Lifeboat Institution is a fictional but highly professional Roblox maritime search and rescue organisation focused on teamwork, realism and training. Members operate lifeboats, deliver casualty care, patrol beaches and engage with the public.

  • Professional training
  • Advanced medic courses
  • Lifeboat & lifeguard operations
  • Friendly community
