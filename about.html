About Us

Professional maritime search and rescue roleplay, inspired by the Royal National Lifeboat Institution.

Who We Are

The LLP Royal National Lifeboat Institution is a fictional Roblox emergency service community dedicated to delivering professional, realistic and enjoyable maritime roleplay. Our members work together across multiple departments to provide lifesaving assistance, emergency medical care, beach safety and public engagement.

Our Mission

Saving Lives

Deliver realistic search and rescue operations whilst maintaining the highest standards of professionalism.

Training

Develop members through structured courses, practical exercises and operational experience.

Community

Build a welcoming environment where teamwork, respect and enjoyment come first.

Our Core Values

Professionalism

We represent the RNLI with maturity, respect and realism.

Teamwork

Every successful rescue depends upon communication and working together.

Integrity

We act honestly, fairly and respectfully both on and off duty.

Commitment

We encourage continuous learning and personal development.

What We Offer

🚤 Lifeboat Operations

Respond to emergency incidents at sea using specialist rescue vessels.

🩺 Medic Training

Learn advanced first aid, casualty care and emergency procedures.

🏖️ Lifeguarding

Patrol beaches, prevent incidents and keep the public safe.

🤝 Community Events

Take part in public engagement, exercises and collaborative roleplay.
